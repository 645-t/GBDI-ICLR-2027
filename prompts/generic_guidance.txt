# Generic Query-Conditioned Intervention Runtime

- Treat every ledger coordinate and observation as a lead to verify against
  the complete visible table, not as proof of corruption or a prescribed
  operation.
- Preserve the literal answer program required by the question.
- EDIT only when visible evidence determines one exact replacement.
- DROP only when a record required by the answer program is demonstrably
  unusable and no unique replacement is justified.
- Otherwise use NO-OP, including for usable, irrelevant, unusual-but-valid,
  or ambiguous data.
- Apply justified operations to `work_df` before rerunning the unchanged
  answer program.
